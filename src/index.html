    Character.AI export: common questions

    The ZIP asks for a password. Where is it?

    The password is the email address your Character.AI account uses, typed in lowercase (for example alex.smith@gmail.com, not Alex.Smith@Gmail.com). The built-in unzip tools on Windows, Mac and phones often can't open this kind of encrypted ZIP even with the right password. Drop the ZIP here instead and type your email when asked — BotToBook opens it in your browser and retries in lowercase for you. If you've already unzipped it, drop message.json (and character.json and user.json if you like) instead.

    Why doesn't my export say who wrote each message?

    The official export lists each message's text, its chat and its time — but not whether you or the character wrote it, and not the character's name. BotToBook works out the sides from the turn order and the reply timing (characters answer within seconds; people take longer). If a chat comes out backwards, press Swap who said what. Then type the character's name once for that chat.

    Are my chats uploaded anywhere?

    No. The ZIP is opened and the book is built by JavaScript running on this page, on your device. There are no trackers or ads. The only thing sent anywhere is a Pro licence key, when you activate it, so it can be checked with the payment provider.

    Is Character.AI shutting down?

    As of late November 2026, Character.AI is in a Chapter 11 sale process that it expects to wrap up in late November or early December, and it has said the platform could be wound down if no suitable buyer is found. Check Character.AI's own announcements for the latest. Either way, requesting your export now and keeping a copy you can read is a good idea.

    How do I read the EPUB on a Kindle?

    Use Amazon's Send to Kindle (by email, in the Kindle app, or on the web) — it accepts EPUB files and converts them for your Kindle. Kobo, Apple Books, Google Play Books, Boox and KOReader open EPUB directly.

    How does the PDF work?

    Press PDF book and a print-ready book opens in a new tab: a cover, then each chat as a chapter, laid out for the page size you picked, with page numbers in Chrome and Edge. Choose Save as PDF in the print dialog (on iPhone: Share → Print, then Share again → Save to Files). Your browser does the typesetting, so every language and emoji comes out right.

    What files does it open?

    The official Character.AI export (ZIP, or message.json / character.json / user.json), CAI Tools exports (JSON and Tavern JSONL), SillyTavern .jsonl chats, and most JSON files that hold a list of messages. When a file does say who spoke, BotToBook uses that instead of guessing.
