How to open your Character.AI export

To open a Character.AI export, download the ZIP from the email within 7 days and unlock it with your account email in lowercase. Your chats are in message.json, a raw data file with no names, so load the ZIP or that file into a browser viewer such as BotToBook to read them as conversations.

Step 1: request the export

On character.ai go to Profile Settings → Account → Manage Account & Data → Export Data. In the app it's Settings → Legal & Privacy → Export Data. Character.AI says the export can take 3–4 weeks; the email's download link works for 7 days, so save the ZIP as soon as it arrives.

Step 2: unlock the ZIP

The ZIP is password-protected. The password is your account email in lowercase — more on the password. Built-in unzip tools often refuse it; a browser viewer can open it directly.

Step 3: read the chats in a viewer

Drop the ZIP (or message.json) on BotToBook. It groups messages into conversations, puts them in order, works out who said what, and lets you type the character's name for each chat. Reading and text downloads are free.

Why the export looks broken

Opened in a text editor, message.json is one long list of every message from every chat, with no sender and no character name. That's how Character.AI's export is built, not a damaged file.

Other free viewers

Several free tools also open the export in the browser, including the open-source viewer by anima-universe on GitHub, Amavie, Yanmate, Neon Bay and chatbotbackup.com. BotToBook's focus is turning the chats into a proper book — a typeset PDF and an EPUB for e-readers.

Questions

Can Character AI chats be exported?

Yes. Character.AI has an official Export Data option under Profile Settings → Account → Manage Account & Data on the web, and under Settings → Legal & Privacy in the app. It emails you a link to a password-protected ZIP of your messages. Character.AI says this can take 3–4 weeks, and the link works for 7 days.

How do I open a Character AI export?

Download the ZIP from Character.AI's email within 7 days. Unlock it with your account email in lowercase, or drop the ZIP on a browser viewer such as BotToBook, which decrypts it on your device. Your chats are inside message.json, a raw data file that needs a viewer before it reads like a conversation.

Why is my Character AI export data not working?

The usual causes are a ZIP password typed with capital letters (it must be your account email in lowercase), a download link that expired after 7 days, or opening message.json directly, which shows raw data with no names or speakers. A viewer such as BotToBook handles the password and rebuilds each chat for you.

What does "Export Data" on Character AI mean?

Export Data is Character.AI's official way to get a copy of what your account holds. You request it in settings, and Character.AI emails a link to a ZIP containing user.json (your profile), character.json (characters you created) and message.json (every message from all your chats). It is raw data, not a readable transcript.

How long does it take for Character AI to export data?

Character.AI says a data export can take 3 to 4 weeks to arrive. When it is ready you get an email with a download link that works for 7 days, so download the ZIP as soon as it arrives and keep a copy somewhere safe. Request it early if you are worried about losing access.

Can I move Character AI chats to another account or app?

As far as we know, Character.AI offers no way to import an export into another account or back into the app. What you can do is keep the chats readable: open the export in a viewer and save them as a PDF, EPUB or text file you control. Some other chat apps also accept SillyTavern-style files.
