How to read your Character.AI chats on a Kindle

To read Character.AI chats on a Kindle, turn your data export into an EPUB and send it with Amazon's Send to Kindle. BotToBook builds the EPUB in your browser from the export ZIP, one chapter per chat with a table of contents. Kobo, Apple Books and Google Play Books open the same file directly.

Make the EPUB

Drop your Character.AI export ZIP on BotToBook (the password is your account email in lowercase), type the character's name for each chat, tick the chats you want, and press EPUB. You get one EPUB 3 file with a cover, a title page and a chapter per chat.

Send it to your Kindle

  • Email: attach the EPUB and send it to your Kindle's Send-to-Kindle address (shown in your Amazon device settings).
  • Kindle app: share the file to the Kindle app on your phone or computer.
  • Web: upload it on Amazon's Send to Kindle page.

Amazon converts the EPUB for your Kindle; it then syncs to every Kindle device and app on your account.

Kobo, Apple Books, Boox, KOReader

These open EPUB directly: copy it to the device, add it to Apple Books, or upload it to Google Play Books.

Long chats

Very long chats are split into parts inside the book, each listed in the table of contents, so pages turn quickly even on older e-readers.

Questions

Can I read my Character AI chats on a Kindle?

Yes. Turn your Character.AI export into an EPUB with BotToBook, then send the file to your Kindle with Amazon's Send to Kindle by email, the Kindle app or the web page. Kobo, Apple Books, Google Play Books, Boox and KOReader open the same EPUB directly, with one chapter for each chat.

Will a very long Character AI chat work on an e-reader?

Yes. BotToBook splits long chats into parts of about 350 messages inside the EPUB, each listed in the table of contents, so pages turn quickly even on older Kindles and Kobos. A chat with thousands of messages becomes one chapter with several parts rather than one huge file.
