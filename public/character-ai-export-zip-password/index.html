What is the password for the Character.AI export ZIP?

The password for a Character.AI export ZIP is the email address of your Character.AI account, typed in lowercase, for example alex.smith@gmail.com rather than Alex.Smith@gmail.com. It is not your login password and it does not arrive in a separate email. If it fails, the cause is nearly always capital letters or the wrong address.

If the password doesn't work

  • Capital letters. Phones capitalize the first letter of a field. Type the whole address in lowercase.
  • A trailing space. Pasting often adds one at the end.
  • The wrong address. Use the email your Character.AI account is registered with — the one the export email was sent to. If you signed in with Google or Apple, try that account's address.
  • Your unzip app. The ZIP is encrypted, and the tools built into Windows, macOS and phones often refuse it even with the right password.

Open the ZIP without an unzip app

You don't need to unzip it. Drop the ZIP on BotToBook and type your email when it asks for the password. The ZIP is decrypted in your browser — nothing is uploaded — and BotToBook trims spaces and retries in lowercase automatically. Every chat then appears in a list, longest first, ready to read.

What's inside once it opens

A folder named after your username with user.json, character.json and message.json. Your chats are all in message.json, as raw data without names. See what message.json contains.
