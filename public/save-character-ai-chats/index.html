How to save your Character.AI chats

The only way to save every Character.AI chat at once is the official Export Data option in settings, which emails you a ZIP of all your messages but can take 3 to 4 weeks. To keep a chat today, open it in a computer browser and use Print and Save as PDF, copy the text, or use a chat-saving browser extension. Do both: request the export now and save your favourite chats by hand while you wait.

Which to use

  1. Request the official export now. On character.ai: Profile Settings → Account → Manage Account & Data → Export Data; in the app: Settings → Legal & Privacy → Export Data. It's the only method that covers everything, and the wait is the long part.
  2. Save your favourite chats by hand while you wait. Print and Save as PDF works in any computer browser with nothing to install. Scroll to the very top first, because long chats load in pieces.
  3. When the export email arrives, download the ZIP within 7 days and keep two copies. Its password is your account email in lowercase.
  4. Make it readable. The export is raw data with no names. A viewer such as BotToBook rebuilds each conversation and can save it as a PDF or EPUB book.

A word on browser extensions

Chat-saving extensions, such as CAI Tools, read chats from your logged-in Character.AI session, so they can see your account while they run. Check what each one asks permission for, and install only ones with a public track record. BotToBook can open CAI Tools exports too.

What BotToBook does and doesn't do

BotToBook doesn't connect to Character.AI or fetch anything: it opens the export you download yourself, in your browser, without uploading it. So it can't speed up the export. Once you have the ZIP, reading every chat and saving text files is free; full PDF and EPUB books are $4.99 once.

Questions

What is the best way to back up Character AI chats?

Request the official export now: it is the only copy that holds every message from every chat on your account. It can take 3 to 4 weeks, so while you wait, save the chats you care about most from the chat page itself, with your browser's Print and Save as PDF or a chat-saving browser extension. When the export arrives, keep the ZIP and a readable copy, such as a PDF or EPUB.

How can I save a Character AI chat right now without waiting for the export?

Open the chat on character.ai in a computer browser, scroll up until the whole conversation has loaded, then use Print and Save as PDF, or copy the text into a document. Browser extensions such as CAI Tools can also save a chat from the open page; they work with your logged-in session, so check the permissions and install only ones you trust. These save one chat at a time, so still request the full export.

How long does it take for Character AI to export data?

Character.AI says a data export can take 3 to 4 weeks to arrive. When it is ready you get an email with a download link that works for 7 days, so download the ZIP as soon as it arrives and keep a copy somewhere safe. Request it early if you are worried about losing access.

Can I save a Character AI chat as a PDF without the export?

You can scroll a chat to the top and use your browser's Print and Save as PDF, or copy the text into a document, but long chats load in pieces and the layout is rough. The official export contains every message, so it is the more complete way to keep a chat.

Is Character.AI shutting down?

Can I move Character AI chats to another account or app?

As far as we know, Character.AI offers no way to import an export into another account or back into the app. What you can do is keep the chats readable: open the export in a viewer and save them as a PDF, EPUB or text file you control. Some other chat apps also accept SillyTavern-style files.
